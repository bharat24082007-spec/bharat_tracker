Remove your Firebase login and all workspace records. This cannot be undone. Export anything you want to keep first.

Permanently delete your account?

Your profile connections, snapshots, tasks, recurring schedules, goals, preferences, and devices will be removed by the worker. A minimal security marker prevents old login tokens from recreating your deleted workspace.

This cannot be undone. You will verify your Google account before the request is sent.
